PROTOCOL FORMAT ANDDESCRIPTION
                               ----- HEADER -----
                               
+ - - - - - - - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - +
| LoRa ADDH       | LoRa ADDL       | LoRa channel    | payload length: 5 bit       | 
| 8 bit           | 8 bit           | 8 bit           | mode: 3 bit                  |
+ - - - - - - - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - +

LoRa ADDH | LoRa ADDL | LoRa channel - are the adresses sent to LoRa receicever 
                                       to start the session

payload length - fixed length of the payload (fixed on a version level, 
                 it can be changed depending on version)

mode - combination of bits sent to specify what modules of system should be enabled
       (camera module, driver module, etc), oriented on a stm32 controller GPIOx system


                                ----- PAYLOAD ----- 

+ - - - - - - + - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - - - +
|  st_l_ax_x  | st_l_ax_y     | st_r_ax_x     | st_r_ax_y     | reserved buttons    |
|  7 bit      | 7 bit         | 7 bit         | 7 bit         | 4 bit               |
+ - - - - - - - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - - - +
| trigger_l   | trigger_r     |  
| 4 bit       | 4 bit         |   
+ - - - - - - - - - - - - - - + 

*note - protocol is written for and tested with PS DualSense controller
st_l_ax_x - left stick, X axis (0-127)
st_l_ax_y - left stick, Y axis (0-127)
st_r_ax_x - right stick, X axis (0-127)
st_l_ax_x - right stick, X axis (0-127)
reserved buttons - reserved memory for 4 buttons (on-off state)
trigger_l - left trigger (0-15)
trigger_r - right trigger (0-15)


                                ----- FOOTER -----

+ - - - - - - +
| CRC packet  |
| 8 bit       |
+ - - - - - - +

CRC packet - control sum of all packet
